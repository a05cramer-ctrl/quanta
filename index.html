Every trade fills the vault.

Each coin has its own vault wallet. 70% of its creator fee lands there on every buy and sell. The vault only pays out at a measurement, to the wallet the qubits pick.

every buy + sell→creator fee→70% to the vault
03 · 4 K · pulse tube

Fees rent real quantum shots.

20% of the fee pays for time on a real quantum computer through Amazon Braket. A draw is one 10-qubit circuit run for 100 shots. On IQM Garnet that costs about $0.45, which covers one draw.

0.30 task+100 × 0.00145 shot=≈ $0.45 / draw
04 · 100 mK · still plate

At :00 the holders are frozen.

At the top of every hour Quanta takes a snapshot of holders. Your tickets equal the tokens you hold, capped at 1% of supply per wallet. The bonding curve, the pool, the creator wallet and Quanta's own wallets don't get tickets.

hold 0.4% of supply→0.4% of the tickets
05 · 15 mK · mixing chamber

Ten qubits, every holder at once.

The circuit puts all 10 qubits in superposition and measures them 100 times. That gives 1,000 bits nobody could have known ahead of time. Not us, not the dev, not the snipers.

// the whole circuit
qubit[10] q;  bit[10] c;
h q;          // superposition
c = measure q;
06 · measurement

The qubits collapse onto one wallet.

The 1,000 measured bits are hashed together with the mint and the hour, and the hash picks one ticket. That wallet gets the whole vault in the same minute. The Braket task ID, the raw shots and the snapshot hash get posted, so anyone can re-run the pick.

Don't trust us. Re-run it.

Every draw posts four things: the Braket quantum task ARN, the raw shots, the holder snapshot hash and the winning ticket. Pull the task from AWS, hash it the same way, and you'll land on the same wallet.

  • Real hardware only. If a device is offline, the draw moves to the next QPU on the list. There's never a classical random fallback.
  • Fixed in advance. The circuit, the shot count and the selection code are public and the same for every coin.
  • Rolls over. If the vault is under 0.1 SOL there's no draw, and it carries into the next hour.
select.jssame for every coin
// 100 shots × 10 qubits = 1,000 quantum bits
const bits   = task.shots.join("");

// bind the bits to this coin and this hour
const seed   = sha256(bits + mint + hourUTC);

// tickets = tokens held at :00, capped at 1% of supply
const ticket = BigInt("0x" + seed) % totalTickets;
const winner = holders.find(h => ticket < h.cumulative);

payout(vault, winner);   // same minute, whole vault
post(task.arn, bits, snapshotHash, ticket);

House rules.

Draws
Every hour at :00 UTC. Coins measured on IonQ Forte draw every 4 hours.
Fee split
70% coin vault, 20% QPU budget, 10% buys and burns $QUANTA. Locked at launch with pump.fun fee sharing.
Tickets
Tokens held at the :00 snapshot. Capped at 1% of supply per wallet. The bonding curve, the pool, the creator wallet and Quanta wallets are excluded.
Minimum
If the vault holds under 0.1 SOL, there's no draw and it rolls into the next hour.
Randomness
Only QPU measurements through Amazon Braket: 10 qubits, 100 shots. If a device is offline, the draw moves to the next device. Never a classical fallback.
Payout
The whole vault goes to the winner in the same minute. No claim step.
Proof
The task ARN, raw shots, snapshot hash and winning ticket are posted on the coin page for every draw.
